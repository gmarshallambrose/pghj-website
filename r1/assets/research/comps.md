# Comp Research — R1 Ranch Pitch

## Sanctuary Camelback Mountain (Paradise Valley / Scottsdale, AZ)
- **~53 acres** — the "residential luxury village" model
- **118 keys**: 110 casitas & suites + 8 private villas
- Spa: indoor & outdoor treatment rooms, meditation garden, reflection pond, therapy pool
- Dining: Elements (American w/ Asian flavors), jade bar
- Pools: several smaller (infinity, lap, west, east) — NOT a single resort mega-pool
- Casita clusters named after gems (Ruby/Gold/Emerald/Amethyst etc.), A–D buildings
- **ADR ~$577 avg** (Sat low ~$438; high ~$1,273)
- Now operated as "A Gurney's Resort"

## Ritz-Carlton, Dove Mountain (Marana / Tucson, AZ)
- **850 acres** total site (resort core ~38 ac); building **310,000 sq ft**
- **253 keys**: 227 hotel rooms (5-story lodge) + 26 detached casitas w/ private patios
- **17,000 sq ft spa** + serenity pool
- **5 dining venues** (incl. full-service upscale restaurant + pool grill)
- **3 pools**, 235-ft waterslide
- Jack Nicklaus Signature golf
- 25 miles of trail (Wild Burro Canyon), public trailhead
- **ADR ~$741 avg** (range ~$308–$938) + $60/day resort fee + $42 valet

## R1 Ranch (4000 E Granite Dells Rd, Payson, AZ 85541)
- **~79.65 acres** (AZ Private Lands overlay), R1 Ranch LLC
- Long E–W rectangle, gentle slope, existing dirt roads + cleared benches
- Surrounded by Tonto National Forest on all sides
- In **Gila County** (not Town of Payson jurisdiction) — zoning/utilities/access via Gila County + Payson coordination
- Adjacent to Rim Club & Chaparral Pines (Arcis-owned) golf communities
