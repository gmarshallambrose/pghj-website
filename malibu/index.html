Methodology · Tax Rates · Market Comps · Sources

This model separates Town of Payson general-fund revenue (sales / lodging tax) from broader community revenue (property tax to schools, county, and fire). The key insight: a resort's Town revenue is driven by the nightly-rental operation, not by unit sales — deeded RV lots and cabins are real-property transfers and carry no sales tax.

Lodging Tax (the engine)

Stays under 30 days are transient lodging. The Town of Payson keeps 8.88% = 3.88% Transaction Privilege Tax + 5.00% bed tax. (The full rate a guest pays is ~15.48%, but state 5.5% and county ~1.10% are not Town revenue.)

Formula: units × % in rental program × nightly rate × occupancy × 365 × 8.88%.

For scale: the Payson Roundup reported vacation rentals already generate roughly $7M/yr in local taxes town-wide. This project adds to that base.

Construction Tax (one-time)

New construction is taxed under the prime-contracting classification. Per A.R.S. § 42-5075 the base is 65% of the contract (35% deemed-materials deduction).

Town rate 3.88% (Ordinance 964, effective May 2024 — raised from 2.88%). Only the hard-construction share of total development cost is taxable; land and soft costs are excluded (adjustable slider, default 70%).

Example: $16M × 70% × 65% × 3.88% = $282K one-time to Town.

Sale & Rental Comps (defaults)

RV lots: Rim Country deeded lots $50K–$63K; Arizona average ~$148K. Modeled in 3 tiers ($60K / $80K / $110K).

Cabins: Payson resort cabins / park-models ~$200K–$350K; modeled $250K (C) / $325K (A).

Nightly rates: Payson cabins average $372/night (luxury $300+, median $199); RV sites ~$55–$95. Occupancy: market average 43–51% (~186 booked nights).

Storage: enclosed RV/boat units ~$40K–$80K to buy.

Property Tax (community)

Formula: assessed value × ratio × combined rate. Modeled at Gila County combined rate 4.5325%, default assessment ratio 16% (Class 1 commercial; second-home / rental classes vary 10–16% — adjustable).

Distributed roughly Payson Unified Schools ~50% / Gila County ~30% / Fire ~12% / Library & other ~8%. Payson levies little to no town primary property tax, so this is community benefit, not Town general fund.

Not captured: Town impact & permit fees and state shared-revenue (population-based distributions of state TPT and income tax) — both additive, not modeled here.

Off-Site Retail Multiplier (estimate)

Beyond on-property spend, guests, relocating owners, and employees spend money in town — restaurants, fuel, groceries, shops — taxed at the Town's retail rate 3.88%.

Formula: (room-nights × visitor spend) + (resident households × spend) + (jobs × spend), all × 3.88%.

The softest layer — it depends on how much spending stays local vs. leaks to Mesa / Phoenix. Fixed comp-based assumptions (not adjustable): $100 off-property per occupied night, 60 relocating-owner households × $15K/yr, and jobs × $12K/yr. Deliberately conservative; shown separately from the hard lodging figure.
