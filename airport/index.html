Methodology · Sources · Who Sets the Values

Property Tax

Formula: Market Value × Assessment Ratio × Combined Rate

Class 1 (commercial / industrial, built improvements): assessment ratio 16% (decreasing to 15% by 2027 per A.R.S. § 42-15001). All 10 concepts modeled at this rate at full build-out.

Class 2 (vacant land before build-out): assessment ratio 15%. Relevant only for Subdivided Lot Sales during the period after we sell finished lots but before buyers build vertically.

Combined Gila County rate: 4.5325% of assessed value, distributed roughly PUSD ~50% / County ~30% / Fire ~12% / Library & other ~8%.

Who decides the value: The Gila County Assessor sets Full Cash Value annually using comps, cost, and income approaches. Limited Property Value (the tax base) can rise no more than 5% per year per A.R.S. § 42-13301. Owner has annual appeal rights.

Sales Tax (TPT) — Construction

Prime contracting classification applies to all new construction (modification). Per A.R.S. § 42-5075, the tax base is 65% of the prime contractor's gross receipts — the remaining 35% is a deemed-materials deduction baked into the statute.

Town of Payson rate: 3.88% (Ord. 964, effective May 2024). State adds 5.6%, County 1%.

Who decides the value: The contract amount between owner and prime contractor sets the base. Contractor files TPT-2 with Arizona DOR; the Town's portion is distributed back to Payson. ADOR audits if pricing looks off-market.

Worked example: A $1.4M Phase 1 contract pays Town $1,400,000 × 0.65 × 0.0388 = $35,308.

Sales Tax (TPT) — Operations

Once a retail / restaurant / services business operates on the parcel, the Town of Payson collects 3.88% of gross taxable receipts — every year, perpetually.

This is where the FBO (Concept 6) and Retail Strip (Concept 8) dramatically outperform the others. Operating TPT compounds. After 10 years, the Retail Strip alone delivers more than $3M to Town coffers.

Industrial uses (warehouse, manufacturing, flex-industrial, hangars, storage condos sold to owners) generally don't generate operating TPT — they pay property tax only. Self-storage rentals are taxable as commercial rentals.

What This Calculator Does Not Capture

Impact & permit fees: Each new building pays Town impact fees (water, sewer, transportation) plus building permit fees. Typically adds 5-10% on top of construction TPT.

Employee spending multiplier: Each job created generates ongoing TPT through worker spending in town. Not modeled.

Phased vacant land: Until buildings rise, lots may be Class 2 (15%). The model assumes full build-out — actual phased revenue ramps over 3-7 years.

Bed tax (5%): Not applicable to any concept here (no lodging proposed), but worth noting as Payson's other major TPT category.
